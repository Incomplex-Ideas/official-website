01 / Philosophy

Technology should feel like common sense. If it needs explaining, we haven't finished building it.

incomplex ideas starts from a simple belief: most everyday problems don't need complicated software. We take an idea, strip it down to what matters, and build it in the plainest, most effective way we can — so it works for nearly everyone, not just the experts.
